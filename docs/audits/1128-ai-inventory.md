## Owner decisions this inventory surfaces
- **Roam chat.** The 07-22 decision keeps roam AI formless and chat-free; the backend hard-codes `chatOn()` false.
  The guide built here therefore shows reviewed text and data-file prompts only, with no avatar or name in roam.
  A free "ask the guide" needs a new compliance gate on the backend first (planning §9.5).
- **Shop NPC, voice.** The app now sends; the backend decides with `feature.flag.shopNpcChat` and the ASR provider.
  Until they are on, players see the server's own message (「店铺分身对话还没开放」 / 「语音识别还没接入，先打字问我」).
