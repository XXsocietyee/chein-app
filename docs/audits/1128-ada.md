# Apple Design Award audit — 2026-11-28 (sweep a4142df0d)

**Question from the owner:** "Do all completed pages meet the Apple Design Award criteria and are they correct?"

**Short answer: No, not yet.** Four flows are close to award level: Welcome, the onboarding fog disc, the city-orienteering/task HUD and the run result screen. They show a clear identity and use native controls well. Most other screens are correct in structure (MP sections, native navigation, both languages fit) but read as **forms and lists, not crafted objects**. They also contradict each other on shared facts: badge totals, stamp looks, currency, date formats and role names. An ADA jury looks first for consistency and finish, and both are missing here.

- **Inputs:** all 108 images in `docs/sweeps/latest/**` on `origin/design/handoff-r2` (en + zh-Hans single shots and contact sheets, 8 lanes). I opened every one.
- **Rules applied:** `docs/briefs/design-principles.md` (P1–P31) and `docs/briefs/round3-1127.md` (OWNER RULE / RULE 2).
- **Code checked:** `origin/ui/combined-r7`.
- **Scope:** read-only. No app code was changed.

> **Note on fixture text.** Strings such as "Synthetic member", "Fixture club 81" and "Sample conversation" come from `#if DEBUG` fixtures (`App/DesignFixtures/DesignFixtureRegistry.swift:1`, `App/MemberHomeFixture.swift:1`). They **will not reach real users** in Release builds. However, (a) these sweeps are what reviewers, the owner and any App Store screenshot pipeline see, and (b) the EN fixtures are poorer than the ZH ones. For example, ZH shows the real sentence "周六一起走了老城线…" where EN shows "Fixture club post" (`App/ClubCommunityFixture.swift:33`). This hides real EN wrapping problems. The table lists them as P2 "fixture hygiene" so they do not drown out real defects.

Score key (1–5): **D** Delight and Fun · **I** Inclusivity · **In** Innovation · **Int** Interaction · **S** Social Impact · **V** Visuals and Graphics.

### The 5 biggest gaps to an award-level app

1. **Collections are not one object (P5, P8, P14, P24).** A badge is a star medal on the badge wall, an orange hexagon on the passport (with *different* glyphs in EN and ZH), and named "First Stamp" in one place and "First Roam" in another. Totals disagree: 3 / 6 on the wall, 3 of 12 on the passport, 2 / 3 in the club. A stamp is a photo circle in the album and a blue 8-point blob on the passport; the album says "3 stamps in total" while showing 2. A jury will find this in seconds.
2. **The core experience, roam, is not shown.** The Roam tab is a drawn placeholder grid with three buttons and no fog, explored area, position or status words (P9, P10). The best idea in the app (onboarding's fog disc) never appears on the map where it should pay off.
3. **Reward and completion moments are flat (P7, P8, P16, P30).** Stamp collected has one exit and a generic pin glyph. Wrap-up has no Share and no route record, and its Done looks disabled. Neither shows visible celebration or uses `.sensoryFeedback(.success)` on appear.
4. **Too many screens are a near-empty list or a form.** Home before load (`ia-tabs`: a grey house glyph, no words), wave-today-club (one card at the top), tickets (two plain rows, no pass, P20), stamp album, enrollment roster, club directory, free-explore pass (six admin sections), order lookup. Each needs either a real empty/loading state or a designed object.
5. **Locale and number formatting is inconsistent.** EN shows `CN¥` on Me, wallet and order (`MemberHomeHeader.swift:638`, `.currency(code:"CNY")`) but `¥` on passes and registration (`E1CommerceComponents.swift:116`, `MPParityPassSession.swift:335` hard-code "¥"). ZH dates are raw `2026-11-06 18:20:00` or `11-07 09:00` while EN uses localized formatters. Count style mixes "3 of 12" (EN) with "3 / 12" (ZH) and "3 / 6". The same concept has different names: Collected/Unlocked (已收集/已点亮), Owner/Creator, Square/动态, Chapters/我的权益.
