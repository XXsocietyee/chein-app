# Native r2 motion r3 — 60 award-level animations (research 11-26)

Owner: 'mainly the animation effects … I need to win the Apple Design Award.' Signature = **ink and light**: the city lights up where you walk. Each row: reference, the motion, the Chengyin moment, the SwiftUI build. Gate with NativeR2Flags.isEnabled("nativeR2.motion3"); Reduce Motion alternative for every row (J section). First release is player + clubs only.

**Signature motion language for Chengyin**
1. **Light is earned, never painted on.** Every brightening (fog burn-off, district colour-up, path glow) is a direct result of walking, scrubbing or punching in, and it follows the drag or the trip in real time. Draws from Monopoly Go cloud fade (1), Moonlitt time scroll (5) and Tide Guide's sky palette (6, 45).
2. **The camera tells the city story.** Big moments are camera moves on the real map (flyover before a race, pull-back for a recap, tilt-in on a teammate) instead of full-screen takeovers. Draws from the WWDC23 route tour (3), Co-Star zoom-out (2) and Wolt 3D tilt (7).
3. **One spring family, bounce only when it's earned.** Default to bounce 0, and add overshoot only when a gesture had momentum or a real milestone happened (punch, medal, rank-up). Draws from Designing Fluid Interfaces (21), Animate with springs (60) and the WWDC23 kudos keyframes (12).
4. **The island is the race's voice.** A single top surface changes shape and stage (start board → leg progress → ring) and expands only for facts that matter, with numbers ticking rather than snapping. Draws from Flighty (15), Citymapper (16) and Design dynamic Live Activities (17, 18).
5. **Every touch has a body: sight, sound and haptic as one event.** Each kit has its own haptic and sound signature tied to cause and speed, with a full Reduce Motion alternative rather than a removal. Draws from WWDC21 audio-haptic design (53), PowerWash per-nozzle haptics (55) and WWDC18 Reduce Motion alternatives (57).
