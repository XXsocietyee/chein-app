## Principles

| # | Principle | What it means in practice | Learned from |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **One glance, one number** | Each surface leads with the single value that matters (distance, minutes left, people in, points), large and monospaced; label small and secondary above it; supporting values in a quiet row below. | Widget sheet (12,572 watching), Fitbit walk (0.01 mi hero), adidas/WHOOP stat rows |
| P2 | **Alive, not static** | Values that change while you look roll (`numericText`), timers tick by themselves (`Text(timerInterval:)`), progress fills; deltas carry color (+318 this hour in green). Nothing looks frozen while something is happening. | Widget sheet, Live Activity references, Strava acquiring-GPS state |
| P3 | **One primary action** | At any moment one obvious next step (Start / 加入 / 开始 / Pause), visually dominant; secondary actions quieter or in a menu. | Step checklist (current step "Start"), Runna (Pause big, Next Lap secondary), GroupMe (I'm in vs Can't go), AllTrails pause FAB |
| P4 | **Progressive disclosure with memory** | Show what's next; fold what's done into a compact "已完成 (n)" with a one-line summary each; a progress ring says "2 of 5". The past stays reachable, never in the way. | Step checklist card |
| P5 | **Same object, every size** | A run, an event, a team, a quest looks recognisably the same as a list row, a card, a detail page, a widget, a Live Activity pill and a notification — same icon tile, color, hero value. Transitions morph between them (`matchedGeometryEffect` / zoom). | Widget sheet (pill/medium/large of one item), shared island |
| P6 | **Other people are present** | Show who is here/going/done: avatar stacks, "3 friends in", host avatar, a friend's name on the live status. Social facts sit next to the action they affect. | Shared island (friends), Partiful guest list, Apple Invites, GroupMe attendees, DICE choose a friend |
| P7 | **Personality at moments, calm everywhere else** | Surfaces are quiet (system backgrounds, one accent per object). Personality — stickers, taped notes, confetti, holo sheen, playful copy — appears at moments that matter: invites, rewards, firsts, completions. | Shared island stickers, Runna 1K badge + confetti, Not Boring unlock, Discord/Cal AI badges, earlier motion r2 refs |
| P8 | **Rewards are objects you keep** | An achievement is a thing (badge/stamp/medal) with a name, the number behind it, and two exits: share it, see the collection. Never a toast that disappears. | Cal AI "First Drop", Discord badge, Runna 1K, Duolingo star |
| P9 | **The map is the stage, the panel is the script** | Map full-bleed; a draggable panel holds live stats and the one action; map controls minimal (MP: no locate button). Route draws in, position pulses. | Strava, AllTrails, WHOOP, adidas, Runna |
| P10 | **Status before content** | Before data is ready, say what's happening in words ("Acquiring GPS", "Writing…", "生成中…"), never a blank or a spinner alone; errors say what to do. | Strava acquiring GPS, step checklist live status |
| P11 | **Invites are cards, answers are one tap** | An invite reads as a small card (what · when · where · who), with two answers and a gentle "later" (Tuck). Saying yes updates everyone's view. | Shared island, GroupMe, Partiful, Apple Invites, Lex RSVP |
| P13 | **Messages can be things** | In chats, plans, polls, invites and game sessions are interactive cards (title, when/where, avatars "2 going", one-tap answer, live result bars); after someone acts, a quiet system line records it ("Alex is going to Mini Reunion"). | Mobbin 11-27: GroupMe event card + "is going" line, Locals poll with voters, Discord game invitation, Beside calendar event, Lex event in group chat |
| P14 | **Show the whole collection** | Collections show every item: earned in full color, unearned as named grey silhouettes with what it takes ("Loch Ness · 100 km"), a count ("1/63 visited"), sections by type or season, and share the whole set. Sequences show numbered slots and a deadline ("2 days left"). | Mobbin 11-27: AllTrails national park stamps, Withings distance badges, Duolingo monthly badges, Lyft locked badges, foodpanda stamp card |
| P15 | **Place facts in one line, actions as a pill row** | A place/venue/checkpoint header reads name → category · area → live status in color ("Open until 6 PM", "Starts in 12 min"), then a horizontal row of capsule actions (Directions · Share · Save · More) instead of stacked buttons; photos as a scrollable strip; social proof ("Saved by 3 friends") right under it. | Mobbin 11-27: Swarm, Apple Maps place card, corner, Beli, Honest Greens |
| P16 | **A finished activity is a named record** | After a walk/run/game: route on the map at top, a title ("Morning Roam", "My first city run"), date · place line, then 3–6 stats in a 2- or 3-column grid with small labels above big values; share + save at the top bar, one Done. Firsts get a celebratory headline (P7). The same record later appears in history unchanged (P5). | Mobbin 11-27: Strava, Gentler Streak, Runna "My First Run", Slopes, Citizen session summary |
| P17 | **A group page answers "what's next, who's here, what's new" in that order** | Club/community home: identity header (cover, name, type · member count, face pile + Invite); then the next event as a card with a live countdown ("In 2m") and its one action (Check in / RSVP); then pinned items (an open poll shows "Vote now · 4 days left"); then the feed with a type filter. One sticky primary action at the bottom ("Post to the club"). | Mobbin 11-27: Meetup home (Incoming + "In 2m" + Check in), Insight Timer group, X community, Glassdoor bowl pinned poll, WhatsApp community |
| P18 | **Search starts before typing** | The empty search screen is useful on its own: "Nearby" first (or a map tile asking for location with the reason), recent searches with per-row delete and "Clear all", then category capsules. Each result row: name, distance · area. Never a blank page under the field. | Mobbin 11-27: Swarm explore, Tripadvisor (Explore nearby + recents with photos), Viator, Skip, Bolt Food |
| P19 | **An inbox is sorted by what needs you** | Message list: large title + search; filter capsules (All · Unread n · Groups · Clubs); each row = avatar/club tile, name, one-line last message prefixed by sender in groups ("Sam: …"), time, unread count badge in the accent; swipe actions Pin · Mute · Archive; pending requests as one row at the top, not mixed in. | Mobbin 11-27: WhatsApp chats filters, GroupMe pending requests + swipe Pin/Archive, Substack unread dots, Slack pins |
| P20 | **A ticket is a pass, not a receipt** | Ticket wallet: Upcoming / Past segmented; the next ticket on top as a pass (cover art, title, date · venue, a notch/perforation edge, count "×2"); detail shows the QR with the activation rule in words ("QR shows 2 h before start"), Send to a friend, Add to Apple Wallet; "Something missing? Find your tickets" as the empty/fallback exit. | Mobbin 11-27: Eventbrite, DICE, Klook, Apple Wallet pass list |
| P21 | **A running activity lives in a mini bar and expands in place** | While a roam / city run / game session / audio guide is running, it stays reachable from every tab as a compact bar just above the tab bar (title, one live value, one control), and tapping it expands that same bar into the full-screen HUD with a continuous morph (`matchedGeometryEffect` / zoom transition); swiping down collapses it back. The tab bar stays; nothing else jumps. Native pattern, not MP chrome (owner rule 2). | Apple Music library → now-playing (via Taste Motion, owner 11-27) |
| P22 | **Profile = identity, one action row, then sections** | Own profile: avatar + name + one line of identity; a stats row (numbers big, labels small, hidden when all zero — matches the MP rule); then ONE row of two equal-width single-line buttons (primary filled + secondary bordered, e.g. Start exploring · Ticket wallet) plus a "···" — never a circle that wraps text; then the section switcher (MP 项目/推文/成就/关于) directly above its content, pinned when scrolling. Achievements show big numbers in a 2-column grid of tappable cards. | Mobbin 11-27: Xbox (Rewards/Posts/Achievements/About — the MP's own reference), Medium (View stats · Edit profile), Sora, komoot stat grid, Digg |
| P12 | **Language-proof layout** | Every element survives English being 2× longer than Chinese and vice versa: wrap, `ViewThatFits`, adaptive stacks, no fixed widths on text. | US launch requirement |

## Motion spec format (owner 11-27, from Taste Motion's "Prompt" breakdown)

## 11-27 17:50 UTC Mobbin round (points history, badge wall)

**P23 — Points history reads as a statement.** (talabat, Subway, Chick-fil-A, PayPal on Mobbin.) The balance and the next expiry sit at the top as one sentence ("39 points · 39 expire on 29 Sep"), then rows grouped under date section headers, each row = source on line 1, time on line 2, signed amount trailing (+ in the system green, − in `.primary`, tabular digits). Why it works: the user's real question is "where did my points come from / go", so the date grouping and the sign carry the meaning and nothing else competes. A help row for missing points ("Not seeing a recent transaction?") sits above the list, not in a menu. Apply to 积分明细 / wallet history **inside the MP's sections and order**: MP tabs map to a segmented `Picker`; the first load stays the MP skeleton.

**P24 — Badge wall shows the whole set, locked ones as silhouettes.** (AllTrails park stamps, Apple Games achievements, ClassPass.) A progress line under the large title ("1/63 parks visited"), every badge in a fixed grid in the same place whether earned or not; locked = the badge's own shape desaturated (`.grayscale()` + lower opacity), not a "?" placeholder; a locked badge with measurable progress shows it (Apple Games ring %, stoic "2/5"). One bottom action at most (Share). Why it works: the empty slots ARE the motivation, and identical shapes make the grid calm. Apply to 徽章墙 / 印章册 (e5b-badge-wall, e2-stamp-album) with MP order and categories kept; dates via `Date.FormatStyle`, names in the UI language.

## 11-27 18:50 UTC Mobbin round (club directory, club detail)

**P25: clubs list splits "yours" from "discover".** (Cal AI Groups, WhatsApp Communities.) The large title, then a "Your clubs" section of plain rows (avatar, name, member count, chevron), then "Discover" rows that carry one trailing bordered `+ Join` button and a one-line description. Why it works: the user's own clubs are navigation (chevron), other clubs are a decision (button), and the two never share a row style. Apply to 查找俱乐部 / club directory inside the MP's sections: the MP's 申请加入 becomes the trailing `.bordered` button; member count as secondary text, not a separate line.

**P26: a non-member's club page states the pitch, then shows a real preview.** (Ladder community join card, AllTrails community feed.) For someone who isn't a member yet, one card explains what the club is for and has a single prominent Join, and real recent posts sit right next to it. Never a "not available in this version" placeholder. Why it works: people join for the content they can see. Apply to the club detail Posts tab (d4-detail): show the MP's posts list, and keep the join CTA as the page's one prominent button.

## 11-27 19:45 UTC Mobbin round (sign-in)

**P27: sign-in is one decision, with only methods that work.** (Lyft, Citizen, Dot, DoorDash.) A left-aligned title that says what happens ("Welcome back" / "Sign up or log in"), the phone field with a country-code menu first (+86 / +1 for our two markets), one primary Continue, then an "or" divider and **Sign in with Apple** in Apple's own `SignInWithAppleButton` style. Terms line in footnote at the bottom. One brand mark at most. Why it works: every visible control leads somewhere; nothing says "unavailable". Apply to login-player: phone first (MP order), Apple below; hide the WeChat row until WeChat login is live; remove the duplicate logo.

## 11-27 20:45 UTC Mobbin round (activity / event detail)

**P28: event facts are icon rows, and the time row has a calendar action.** (AllTrails hike, Google Calendar, Snapchat plan, Partiful.) Under the title: one row per fact, each a leading SF Symbol + value (calendar = date and time range; mappin = place, tappable to Maps; person.2 = "24 going" with stacked avatars, tappable to the list). The date row carries a trailing bordered "Add to Calendar" button (EventKit `EKEventEditViewController`). The price and primary action sit in a bottom bar ("From ¥58 · Register"); for an ended activity the bar says "Ended" with the MP's after-event action (photos / review) instead of a disabled button. Why it works: facts scan as a list, every fact is also a door, and the bar never shows a dead control. Apply to activity detail (rep3-activity-detail, t5-baoming) in the MP's section order.

## 11-27 21:40 UTC Mobbin round (map + nearby sheet)

**P29: the map tab's sheet states a count and filters with chips.** (OpenTable "331 restaurants", Klook Transport/Attractions chips, AllTrails "7 / 19" pager, Apple Maps.) The sheet's collapsed detent shows one line: the count of what is nearby ("2 nearby activities") with a grabber, so the map stays the hero. Medium detent: a chip row (MP categories) then rows with distance + walk time and a trailing directions button; tapping a pin selects its row and pages "n / N" through pins. The sheet never goes to the full detent on its own. Why it works: the map answers "where", the sheet answers "what and how far", and one gesture moves between them. Apply to Roam (d2-roam / d6-roam "附近活动") in the MP's content: `.presentationDetents([.height(…), .medium, .large])`, `.presentationBackgroundInteraction(.enabled(upThrough: .medium))`, distances via `MeasurementFormatter`/`Measurement<UnitLength>` in the user's locale (m/km in CN, mi/ft in US).

## 11-27 22:40 UTC Mobbin round (run / game results)

**P30: the result screen is a moment, then a door.** (BitePal goal reached, LinkedIn Wend "See you tomorrow", Brilliant "Correct! +15 XP", DoorDash trivia.) One big number (time or score) with its unit, a single sentence that says what it means ("41% of new players couldn't solve this"), the start→end facts in a two-column row, then the social and next actions: Share as a bordered capsule beside the primary "Done"/"See the leaderboard". A short celebration (confetti / symbol bounce) plays once, Reduce Motion gets a fade, and a success haptic fires on appear. The leaderboard is a preview with the player's own row highlighted, not a separate empty page. Why it works: the player gets the feeling first, the proof second, and one clear next step. Apply to the wrap-up / 结算 (t5-settle) and the roam finish record (P16) in MP order: `.sensoryFeedback(.success, trigger:)`, `symbolEffect(.bounce)` on the check, `ShareLink` for the result card.

## 11-27 23:40 UTC Mobbin round (post composer)

**P31: the composer is the text field; everything else is an accessory.** (Flo "New post", YouTube "Create post", TikTok post options, Thumbtack.) A sheet with Cancel (leading) and Post (trailing, `.borderedProminent`, disabled until there is content), the author row with an audience menu chip ("Public ▾"), then the text field focused on open. Photos, location and tags sit in a small toolbar above the keyboard (`.toolbar { ToolbarItemGroup(placement: .keyboard) }`) and selected photos show as a removable thumbnail strip. Secondary settings (location suggestions, who can view, share to) are list rows after the text, never before it. Drafts save on Cancel with a confirmation dialog ("Save draft / Delete / Keep editing"). Why it works: the user came to write; the field is ready, and nothing else asks for a decision before the first word. Apply to the club post composer ("有什么新鲜好玩的分享吗?") and 发布 flows in MP field order.

**P32: checkout is one decision, and the button is the payment method.** ([Luma](https://mobbin.com/screens/6490283f-b69b-459c-b8e0-ef95ec4ee1bc), [DICE](https://mobbin.com/screens/c72918d9-c245-44e9-aa9d-835e3aa1607d), [Eventbrite](https://mobbin.com/screens/bd86173f-5c38-4171-a411-f7763f0da4c3), [Posh](https://mobbin.com/screens/5dcdfba7-b84f-420a-9292-62778a069934); counter-examples [StubHub](https://mobbin.com/screens/13940582-24d8-4e10-9416-0b120d758ea4), [Expedia](https://mobbin.com/screens/c30cccfa-cd93-4e0a-802a-85662d2a2e6f).) The event card, ticket line, ONE "Payment method" row showing the chosen method's mark (tap → sheet to change), and a large total fit on one screen. The primary button is the method itself (the black Apple Pay button), so "how" and "pay" are one tap. StubHub and Expedia stack 4-6 radio rows and read like a form, not a purchase. Why it works: the player confirms one thing and the price stays in view. Apply to the registration sheet, pass/bundle buy and order-detail Pay, in MP order: fee details → **"Pay with" row** (CN: WeChat Pay default, then Alipay; US: Apple Pay default when `PKPaymentAuthorizationController.canMakePayments()`, else Card) → total right-aligned with `.monospacedDigit()` → consent footnote → button labelled by the method ("Pay ¥12.50 with WeChat Pay" / `PayWithApplePayButton` / "Pay with Alipay"). Change the method in a `.presentationDetents([.medium])` sheet with an inline `Picker`. Never use a disabled button as an instruction; put the reason in a footnote above it.

**P33: sign-in leads with the market's main method, has one primary action, and gives alternatives equal weight.** ([Lyft](https://mobbin.com/screens/c627d05e-442a-490b-ab0b-60b3dd6e5e4f), [Bolt](https://mobbin.com/screens/240e0527-a9d2-46b5-a50f-f58f538a52f0), [DoorDash](https://mobbin.com/screens/e313817c-191c-443e-afd7-3be2b2b7da18), [Pi](https://mobbin.com/screens/be53cb6a-842c-4c12-81dc-278bb8c7d98a).) Lyft and Bolt open on the phone field with a flag and country-code picker defaulted from region and one "Continue". Then an "OR" divider and full-width, equal-height alternatives with brand marks. Sign in with Apple is never smaller than the others, and terms are a footnote, not a checkbox wall. Why it works: most people tap the first option, so it must be the one most of them use. Apply: China order is WeChat (only when installed) → phone +86 → Sign in with Apple; US order is Sign in with Apple → phone +1. Use the system `SignInWithAppleButton` (style follows colour scheme) at 50 pt, with all alternatives the same size. Phone uses `.textContentType(.telephoneNumber)` + `.keyboardType(.phonePad)`, and the code field uses `.textContentType(.oneTimeCode)` for SMS autofill. Terms and privacy go in a `.footnote` with inline links.

**P34: level B pages are crafted with objects, not ornaments.** ([Luma](https://mobbin.com/screens/9af424b7-5e7f-4619-af62-25a763624cee), [Meetup](https://mobbin.com/screens/6dc52154-97d5-4988-8955-6dac3cf75475), [Partiful](https://mobbin.com/screens/66a726cd-b622-442c-95ec-c5c030b8bca8), [Posh](https://mobbin.com/screens/1177d96b-90b8-441b-bfa2-c6717f9f3615), [Rodeo](https://mobbin.com/screens/5de27d99-db77-4c61-98fe-79cbef1652a1); [Nike Run Club](https://mobbin.com/screens/6d298284-0016-475e-b33e-2d67e78515fe), [Any Distance](https://mobbin.com/screens/7346081c-7908-4702-b447-3fed9bc75611), [Strava](https://mobbin.com/screens/f95f9962-1917-49bd-95f8-95b30ff3f833).) The best event pages are standard navigation and text. They feel designed because of four things: (1) the event's own art fills the top; (2) people are visible ("+331 guests are going", a face pile, "Zoda and 1,025 others going"); (3) the place is a map snippet, not an address line; (4) one floating action bar holds the state and the main action ("You're going! +1 · Ticket"). Nike Run Club makes achievement feel earned with an object: a medal with a metal tier and a level card with one huge number in the level's colour and a progress bar to the next milestone. Strava stays clean but gives the week a chart with an emphasised endpoint. Why it works: character comes from the content made visible (art, people, place, progress), so the page stays professional and still looks like this app. Apply to every level B page: hero art, face pile + social sentence, map snippet, floating action bar, one collection object per concept, big number + unit + progress to the next step.

**P35: AI guide, AI NPC and weather surfaces are useful from the first frame and stay in context.** ([Booking](https://mobbin.com/screens/ee0f45bb-9e36-44e5-b1fa-f3daeefa05dd), [Agoda](https://mobbin.com/screens/70bedca1-6d20-4d84-83ad-10b41b0101bc), [Wanderlog](https://mobbin.com/screens/68c8eb0d-d089-4f90-a427-f6daf12aa384), [Klook](https://mobbin.com/screens/ad9c1616-2e4d-4b7e-9d25-c4cd969ea15b), [Apple Maps](https://mobbin.com/screens/f9c29798-b0d0-4173-9e83-be742559a80a), [Apple Weather](https://mobbin.com/screens/7463e436-f040-46e2-aac0-c3335f8d0e56), [AllTrails](https://mobbin.com/screens/0a061cc5-6d34-49b7-8155-d16e55992a0c).) The guide never opens on an empty chat. It shows a one-line greeting plus 3 starter prompts tied to the player's city and quest. The AI disclosure appears once, inline, in small type, never as a modal. Answers that name places, quests or events include native rich cards (image, name, distance, one action) inside the reply. On the map the guide is a sheet with a scope line ("Asking about: Shanghai · Change"), as in Wanderlog. NPC chat uses the same bubbles plus the character portrait and name in the header, and its lines come from Resources/Story data. Overlays sit behind a layers button, as in AllTrails. Rain, snow, fog and dusk effects are level A only (map and games), and with Reduce Motion they become a static tint plus the chip.

**P36: the ticket wallet is a stack of the event's own art; each ticket answers "when, where, show it".** ([Eventbrite](https://mobbin.com/screens/0d04ebee-04f4-44ce-95f5-9d388307461a), [Venmo × Ticketmaster](https://mobbin.com/screens/9aa32ade-e3b5-4ff9-81ad-7ad8e8ef6c89), [Nike Run Club](https://mobbin.com/screens/b80cc60b-a945-4d7e-a4f8-7c6849ab30d3), [Apple TV](https://mobbin.com/screens/a73255f0-237f-45ac-aa01-053bac911cdc).) Good wallets never show a generic ticket glyph. Each row is the event's cover: full width for the next ticket, a thumbnail for later ones. Date and time come first, then the title and the venue, plus a QR or count badge in the corner. Eventbrite splits Upcoming and Past and gives the next ticket a perforated "ticket" edge.
