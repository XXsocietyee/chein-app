# Getting started as a player

Chengyin is city exploration: routes, activities, check-in, and the record you keep afterward. This page is the player path already described for the mini-program: home, the three play modes, a route or activity, sign-up, and Me.

You can look around before you sign in. Signing in does not have its own registration form. The server creates a player account the first time a sign-in succeeds. Your role still comes from the server.

## Look around first

Home is the first tab. The greeting uses your nickname, or 旅人 when you do not have one yet. The city control falls back to 选择城市 and 全国.

Three fixed entries sit on the home hero:

- 成为俱乐部 — start the club-host application. See the club guide.
- 成为商家 — start the merchant application. Choosing it does not make the account a merchant.
- 和朋友报名活动 — open the activity list.

Under those, the home shelves are 精选路线, 近期活动, 热门主题, and 现成玩法. An empty shelf, a loading shelf, and a failed shelf stay different. The list ends with · 没有更多了 · when there is nothing further to load.

Search is one place. You can switch between a list and a map of the same query, including a city scope and a nearby scope. Search keeps the keyword, category, dates, and price range while you switch. Result groups are 全部, 路线, 活动, 商家, 俱乐部, and 动态. Recent searches keep up to ten distinct keywords.

The player tab bar is 首页, 漫游, 模板 / 玩法, 俱乐部, and 我的.

Declining the privacy gate keeps guest browsing. Guest browsing can open home. Orders, the ticket wallet, clubs you joined, and other private lists ask you to sign in.

## Sign in when the app asks

The mini-program signs in with WeChat in the background. The iOS app has no silent credential, so it shows a sign-in screen.

Use the phone code when that screen is shown. The code is six digits. You send it yourself, then wait out the cooldown before sending another. There is no separate sign-up page: a new phone number becomes a player account on the server.

Other sign-in buttons appear only when that build has turned them on. A missing button is not available. This guide does not describe password sign-in.

If a saved session expires, the app asks you to sign in again or to keep browsing as a guest. Signing out is 退出账号 in Settings. It does not show that expiry prompt.

Before a write that needs agreement, the app links the user agreement and the privacy document. Those documents are the placeholder above until approved text is supplied. The privacy guide body is still not bundled, so the privacy screen can show an unavailable state. That is expected.

## Three ways to play

Open 城瘾玩法 from Settings, or the play guide from Me. The title in Chinese is 城瘾玩法. The page says: three ways to play, ready when you are. You do not study a rulebook first.

| Mode | Tag | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classic | Group · In order | Check in stop by stop. Everyone sets out together at the start time. Fits a meetup or a story route. |
| Free exploration | Anytime · On your own | Every stop is open. Start where you like. A pass lets you play without waiting for a full group. |
| Roam | No finish · Stroll | Light up the city fog as you walk. No tasks and no finish line. A stroll on the way counts. |

经典 and 自由探索 open Home. 漫游 opens the Roam tab. Each row has 去看看.

玩法文档 lists articles the server has published. An empty list says 暂无玩法说明. A removed article says it is no longer available. Those states are not the same as a network error.

On a route, the type line is 自由探索 or 城市定向. Sections are 详情, 路线节点, and 路线. You will also see the meeting place, what is included, the lineup, estimated time, distance, and participating shops. If the publisher has not finished the stops, the route says so instead of inventing stops.

## Join an activity or open a route

From a route or activity, the main button depends on the server:

- 立即报名 when sign-up is open.
- 查看场次 when you need to pick a session. A session line shows the name, date, time, and price.
- 随时开玩 for a pass you can start on your own.
- 招募中 · 未开售, 定价中 · 未开售, or 立项待审 · 未开售 when it is not on sale yet.
- 去票夹 · 查看我的票 when you already hold a ticket.
- A closed-merchant line when the shop has paused and sign-up is paused with it.

Some activities are limited to a club. The screen then asks you to join that club first. Seats can also be held for members, so a public seat can be gone even when a number still looks available.

An explore pass is one ticket per order, and one live ticket on the same route for one account. The quote says so when a second one is refused.

Some older four-shop products no longer take new sign-ups. A ticket that was already sold still follows its existing contract. The screen states that. It does not open a new sale.

Paid sign-up shows a quote before you confirm. The quote says whether you can still refund. The usual cutoff is 24 hours before the gathering time. After that cutoff, the quote says this order cannot be refunded. Read that line before you confirm.

If the server says online payment is not available and tells you to contact the organizer, stop there. This guide does not add a payment address or a card form. Virtual goods are not sold here.

After a paid sign-up, the ticket lives in the ticket wallet. A pass can show how many days are left, including 今天过期 when it ends today. My orders is the place to find a ticket you already bought. The topic page says 已购票？去「我的订单」找回.

Cancel is offered for a pending order, or for an order that has not started or is still in progress, when the screen shows it. A paid selection follows the cancel-and-refund path the server returns. It is not a silent cancel. The host of an activity can cancel the whole activity. That refunds paid players, voids tickets already issued, and takes the activity off the list. That action belongs to the host, not to a player.

Participants you save are for sign-up contact and on-site check. The form says they are not shown publicly and are not used for delivery.

## While you play

Play keeps the registration you entered with. The ticket detail for that registration is the one attached to the route, not a different merchant record with the same number.

A story route shows chapters and stops in order. It stops at the first stop that is incomplete, locked, or not yours to see yet. It does not reveal a thought you have not earned. Sound waits until you choose narration, a guide, or a passage. It does not start by itself.

Check-in is part of classic play: you arrive at the stop, the app explains why it needs location, and only then asks. A challenge at a stop can ask for a photo or a short answer. The server decides whether the check-in counts and whether a medal is awarded. The app does not invent that result.

When a run ends, the route can show a recap: completed, ended, or still in play, plus the result card when the server sends one. You can write a review afterward. The empty review line says nobody has reviewed the route yet.

Roam has no finish line. Walking lights the fog. Coupon codes, when you hold one, are shown to a shop to scan. That is an in-store check, not a digital item for sale.

## Me, settings, and safety

Me shows your name, level, city, and streak when those numbers are ready. From there:

- 票夹 — tickets you hold.
- 我的订单 — sign-ups and their status.
- 我的项目 — routes, activities, and templates you published. See the creator guide.
- 我的资产 — balance when the server sent one. A missing balance stays a dash. It is not zero.
- 我的收藏 — posts and routes you saved.
- 城瘾玩法 — the three modes and the play docs.
- Settings — profile, club host, merchant, favorites, privacy and location, the user agreement, account cancellation, about, and sign out.

Profile edits cover nickname and the city line. Avatar and phone binding stay on the screen the app actually offers. If an upload or a binding is unavailable, the screen says so.

Privacy and location includes your own media waiting for review, and the nearby-people switch. If that switch cannot be submitted, the app says the version cannot submit it and that nothing changed.

You can report or block from the menu next to content, when that menu is shown. The app does not publish a moderation deadline here.

Account cancellation is its own flow. It is not the same as signing out. Follow the screen. This guide does not restate the cancellation notice.

## When a screen will not move

- Loading, empty, and error are different. Retry the error. An empty list has nothing to retry.
- A 401 means the session expired. Sign in again. A 403 means this account cannot see that page. Signing in again does not fix a 403.
- If the result of a sign-up or a cancel is unknown, do not send it again. Open the order or the ticket and read what the server shows now.
- Location is asked only for the step that needs it, after a short explanation. Declining location on a merchant place picker still lets you fill in the other fields. A check-in that needs a location cannot be completed without it.
