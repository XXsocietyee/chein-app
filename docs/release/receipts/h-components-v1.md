## 3. Token Hygiene Fixes Landed Alongside Components

- **Removed slate-blue `0x0F172B` light card shadow**: `CYDomainPalette.cardShadowColor` migrated to neutral `CYRGB(hex: 0x000000, alpha: 0.05)`; light `sheetCard` nested surface aligned to `#F8F9FA` (`CYDomainPalette.swift`).
- **Corrected tab bar chrome**: `CYTabBarAppearance` unselected alpha aligned from `0.45` to `Theme.MiniProgramChrome.tabUnselectedOpacity` (`0.6`), and `CYDomainTabBarStyle` player mode switched from `.ultraThinMaterial` blur to top-fade gradient (`0 -> bgPage`) in `CYDomainTabBar.swift`.
- **Toast & Secondary/Destructive buttons**: Removed `borderStrong` stroke from toast pill (`CYSharedStates.swift`); updated `CYDestructiveButtonStyle` to solid `actionDangerBg` (`#D0323B`) with `actionDangerFg` (`#FFFFFF`) and disabled buttons to `bgSurfaceSubtle` + `textPlaceholder` (`CYDesignComponents.swift`).
