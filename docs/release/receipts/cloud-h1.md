## Decisions where the docs and the mini-program disagree (the mini-program wins, per the brief)

1. **No city picker.** §三 U02-02 asks for a keep-places vs change-city choice. The owner later ruled "不需要选择城市 / 不需要换城市" (mini r2-create b4-u02-02/04). The app never sends `NEW_CITY`. It explains in-line that places are kept and get replaced in Place to-dos.
2. **Conditions without a fact are omitted**, not shown as "待补充" (r2-create b4-u02-01).
3. **Place to-do rows:** the owner said "有点乱" and the mini dropped the observation lines. The app shows "look at / what to do / if unavailable" only when the contract actually has text. Derived slots are blank, so normally nothing extra shows, and the doc's requirement still holds for authored contracts.
4. **requestId:** the mini sends no requestId on `/use`. The app sends one because the backend supports receipt replay and the brief requires requestId kept on retry with a receipt re-read.
