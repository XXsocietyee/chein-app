## Who this covers

People who explore, post, comment, message, and take part in clubs and activities in Chein. It also covers merchants and club organizers when they post in those same spaces.

## Operating notes

These are house rules for answers. They are not the UGC policy.

- Treat other people with respect. Harassment, threats, and hateful attacks are not acceptable.
- Do not post someone else's private information, including a phone number, an address, or an identity document.
- Do not post sexual content involving a minor. Report it. Support does not ask for a copy of that content.
- Do not spam, scam, or impersonate the app, its staff, or another person.
- Do not use community spaces to sell virtual goods or to collect bank-card details. Both stay off in 0.1.0 (1).
- Location is used only while the app is open and when the person chooses a location feature or starts personal sharing with someone they picked. Do not ask anyone to leave location running in the background for an unrelated reason.

## Reporting and blocking

Use a report action only when the screen already shows one.

- Square posts and comments have Report in the overflow menu. Block from a post tells the person it is not available yet and that nothing was blocked. Say that. Do not say the block succeeded. The post-block notice also says they can report the content, or block the person from a chat when that chat shows the action.
- Club channels already show report actions for a channel, a message, and a member. Use those. Do not treat the rules sheet on that screen as this document's UGC policy, and do not repeat a clock time from it.
- Club posts and comments, and merchant reviews, use the report action already on that screen.
- In Messages, report and block exist only on conversations that already offer them. A notice that only offers hide stays that way.

These screens have no report entry. Do not invent a button:

- Topic detail
- Topic reviews and sections
- Activity reviews
- Club post discussion comments
- Member avatars

For those, the person describes the content to support. Support writes it down. Support does not promise that it will be removed, and does not give a time, because review staffing is inside `[UGC_POLICY_PLACEHOLDER]`.

Where block is shown and the screen completes it, it applies to that person's own view. It is not, by itself, a ban for everyone else. Do not say someone was banned unless an owner process has recorded that. This document does not define that process.
